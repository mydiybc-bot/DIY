Welcome! Here's how it works

You bake it yourself, step by step, with the recipe on a tablet. Our staff are here if you need help.

3 steps

  1. 1Choose a dessert

    Pick one from the menu. You pay per dessert.

  2. 2Bake with the tablet

    Follow the recipe and get the ingredients yourself. English recipes are available.

  3. 3Clean, pack, take home

    Wash your tools, put your dessert in the box and take it home.

Price

You only pay the dessert price on the menu. No service fee.

NT$149

Up to 2 people per dessert. A second person who does not make their own dessert pays NT$149.

  • 2 people, 2 desserts = dessert price × 2
  • 2 people, 1 dessert = dessert price + NT$149
  • 3 people = at least 2 desserts

Pay at the counter on the day: cash or LINE Pay. Credit cards only at some stores. Please ask our staff.
